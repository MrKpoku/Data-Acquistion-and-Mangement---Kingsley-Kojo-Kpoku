CONVERTING THE DATA COLUMNS

ny_housing_long <- readr::read_csv(
  "Project 2 - NY_Housing.csv",
  show_col_types = FALSE
) |>
  dplyr::mutate(
    dplyr::across(
      -c(Year, Month),
      ~ readr::parse_number(as.character(.x))
    )
  ) |>
  tidyr::pivot_longer(
    cols = -c(Year, Month),
    names_to = "Borough",
    values_to = "Average_Price"
  )

COMPARE YoY GROWTH

yearly_summary <- ny_housing_long |>
  dplyr::group_by(Year, Borough) |>
  dplyr::summarise(
    Average_Price = mean(Average_Price, na.rm = TRUE),
    .groups = "drop"
  ) |>
  dplyr::arrange(Borough, Year) |>
  dplyr::group_by(Borough) |>
  dplyr::mutate(
    YoY_Growth_Rate = (Average_Price / dplyr::lag(Average_Price) - 1) * 100
  ) |>
  dplyr::ungroup() |>
  dplyr::arrange(Year, dplyr::desc(Average_Price))

CONCLUSION AND ANALYTICS FINDING

1.Average housing prices increased across all five boroughs from 2024 to 2025. Staten Island had the highest year-over-year (YoY) growth rate at 5.4%, while Manhattan had the lowest. This shows that prices grew fastest in Staten Island in this dataset; price growth alone does not show that more residents moved there.

2. The monthly line graph shows Brooklyn with the highest average prices and the Bronx with the lowest. Prices generally trended upward across the boroughs, although monthly changes varied. These price patterns do not indicate how many people moved to each borough.

3. Real estate Analyst Advice
As a real estate analyst, I would advise customers who are interested in Brooklyn to consider purchasing sooner rather than waiting, because the sample shows its average prices rising over time. However, this is a pattern in the dataset, not a guarantee that prices will continue to rise.

Manhattan remains the most expensive borough, as expected, so customers considering it should plan for a higher budget and compare housing options carefully. Staten Island had the highest year-over-year growth rate in the sample, suggesting that its prices increased the fastest from 2024 to 2025 and may continue to attract buyer interest. These recommendations are based on simulated data, so customers should check current market information before making a real purchase.
